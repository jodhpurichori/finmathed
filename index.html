What is Transfer Cost Recovery?

It is the estimated time required for monthly EMI savings to recover the processing, stamp and miscellaneous charges paid for transferring the loan.

Example: If transfer charges are ₹60,000 and monthly EMI saving is ₹3,000, estimated recovery is 20 months.
